Research interests

Research statement ↗

My research interests lie in LLM post-training and efficient LLM, with a focus on making large language models more capable, reliable, and deployable.

01

LLM post-training

SFT, reinforcement learning, and training efficiency.

02

Efficient LLM

Inference acceleration via quantization, pruning, and distillation.

03

Continual learning

Fine-tuning pretrained LLM and VLM on downstream tasks.

04

Trustworthy AI

Privacy-preserving machine learning and model safety.
